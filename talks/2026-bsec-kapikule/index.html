The problem

  • Long truck queues, up to 11 km, especially on weekends
  • Paperwork and border inspection make up 13% of total goods transport cost
  • Every 10% increase in time cuts trade volume by 5–25%
  • Average transit time Turkey → Germany, France or Poland is about 4 days (96 h)

Conclusions

1,000 runs

Simulation is a powerful tool

Discrete-event simulation in Arena captured real-world complexity, with 1,000 runs over 48-hour windows for robust results.

2% vs 83%

The root cause is operations, not infrastructure

Only 2% of total transit time is actual customs processing; 83% is queuing. Staff scheduling matters more than building more lanes.

+44.5%

Best import scenario (IS7)

Extra passport lane, integrated station, 7 registration desks and 24/7 livestock officers: the queue falls 67%.

+48%

Best export scenario (ES13)

Requires Bulgarian cooperation, a single station, 24/7 staffing and an expanded truck park: the queue falls 74%.

≥ 5%

The trade impact is substantial

A 10% transit time reduction brings a 5–25% trade volume gain. Turkey’s exports to Europe are expected to rise at least 5% from Kapıkule improvements.

Replicable

The framework travels

The methodology applies to Kapitan Andreevo (Bulgaria), other BSEC corridor crossings and Eastern Partnership borders.
